Lugano Orders

The order booking app of Lugano. Our salesmen and the shops we supply use it to see what is in stock and to place orders for men's and kids' ethnic wear: sherwanis, kurtas, blazers and more.

Live stockEvery design with its colours, sizes and sets, updated from our stock records through the day.
Orders and requestsSalesmen book orders for shops; shops send order requests that our office confirms.
ListsDesigns picked for a shop, shared as a link or a PDF with photos.

Who can use it

The app is for Lugano's staff and for shops we do business with. Our office adds staff and customers; anyone else only sees the lists we send them and can send us an order request.

Signing in

You sign in with your Google account. The app asks Google only for your email address, so it knows who you are and what you may see. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account. See our privacy policy for the details.

To open the app, use the link Lugano sent you, or open it here. For help, contact your Lugano salesman or our office.

Install the app

Put Lugano Orders on your phone's home screen. It opens full screen like any other app, and Lugano links you receive open in it.

Lugano Orders is installed on this phone. Open it from the home screen.

Android phones (Chrome)

Open app.lugano.in/app in Chrome and tap Install on the black bar at the top. If you don't see it, tap Chrome's ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

iPhone (Safari)

Open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Trouble opening the app?

If Google shows "Sorry, unable to open the file at present", your browser is signed in to more than one Google account. Open the link in a private (incognito) tab and sign in with just the account you use for Lugano, or use a browser that has only that account. This is a known Google issue; it doesn't happen with one Google account on the phone.
